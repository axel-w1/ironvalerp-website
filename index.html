GOOD TO KNOW

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS.

Is Ironvale RP an 18+ community?

Yes. Ironvale's published community rules specify an 18+ requirement.

Can I join if I'm new to FiveM roleplay?

New roleplayers are welcome to explore the community. Read the server rules and use the official Discord support channels if you need help getting started.

How do I apply for SASP, EMS, Fire or DOJ?

Check the official Discord for current department recruitment, eligibility and application instructions.

Can I start a business or faction?

Check the current requirements and contact the appropriate coordination team through official community channels.

Where can I report a bug or request support?

Use the official Ironvale Discord ticket system. Never send passwords or other private account credentials in a ticket.
